==================== SLIDE 5 ====================
[1] 04 • WHY FFT
[2] Why Fourier Transform?
[3] The same vibration can be difficult to interpret in time, but clearer in frequency
[6] Time domain
[7] Shows vibration amplitude versus time.Useful for overall signal shape, but fault-specific frequency components may be difficult to separate.
[101] Frequency domain
[102] Shows how vibration energy is distributed across frequencies.Characteristic peaks become easier to observe.
[103] Frequency spectrum
[109] Low frequency → High frequency
[112] Diagnostic value
[113] • 1X rotational peak → imbalance• High-frequency impacts → bearing fault• Frequency features → ML inputs
[114] FFT turns a complex waveform into a frequency map that is easier to quantify.
